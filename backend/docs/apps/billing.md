## Rules worth knowing (the fields are in `models/`; the error codes in `errors/`)
- **`CondominiumBillingRules`** (1:1 with `Condominium`, spec 041; it was `condominiums.BillingRules`): how a condominium is billed. `due_day` 1–28, discount 0–100 and (spec 020) the late-payment rules. For a **reference month** (the month an issuance refers to), `due_month` (`reference` | `following`) says where the due date falls and `competence_month` (`same_as_due` | `before_due`) which month the boleto accounts for (`due_month_of`, `competence_month_of`). The fine is `late_fee_percentage`, at most 2 % (Civil Code art. 1.336 §1), and an optional first-days tier (`initial_late_fee_percentage` and `initial_late_fee_days`, both or neither: `CondominiumBillingRulesRules`). The interest is counted as `full_month` or `per_day` (`interest_calculation`) after `interest_grace_days`; `should_show_qr_code` and `should_show_fee_statement` are boleto options. The monthly issuance (spec 026) reads `issuance_day` (1–28, default 1: the day of the month it runs for this condominium), `exempts_manager_unit` (default false: leave the manager unit out), `due_date_of(reference_month)` (the due day of the due month, a Saturday or Sunday moved to the next Monday when `due_on_business_day`; holidays are not considered) and the late-payment terms it copies onto each document; the other fields (QR code and statement options, `credit_limit`) are still not read. Its `condominium` cannot change once saved. A condominium without rules is skipped by the issuance (`no_billing_rules`).
- **`LateInterestRate`** (spec 041; it was in `condominiums`): the monthly interest of a condominium from a start date, one per `(condominium, start_date)`; `condominium.late_interest_rates.in_force_on(date)` is the rate with the latest start not after `date`, `None` before the first (no interest).
- **`BillingPlan`** (the PDF's *PlanoCobranca*): a named plan of a condominium (unique name per condominium), `active`, optional `start_month` / `end_month` (first day of a month; the end not before the start). Its `condominium` never changes after creation. `BillingPlan.objects.in_force(reference_month)` is the active plans whose months include the reference month; the monthly issuance joins the items of all of them into **one** boleto per unit.
- **`BillingPlanItem`** (*ItemPlanoCobranca*): `description`, `cost_center` and `entry_template` (both active; the cost center of the plan's condominium, the template of that cost center; `PROTECT`), `calculation_kind` and only that kind's fields: `fixed_amount` → `amount` per unit; `ideal_fraction_share` → `amount` is the condominium's total, split by `apportionment_criterion` (a `condominiums.CondominiumApportionmentCriterion` of the plan's condominium, spec 047; before it the item named a number from 1 to 6); `unit_registered_value` → `unit_value_field` (`gas`, `water`, `electricity`: the unit's own value), no amount. Each kind rule is in `BillingPlanItemRules` (the criterion rules, including the plan's condominium, are model rules only: the database keeps no check on the criterion, so the previous release can still write items during a deploy) and, for amount and unit value, a check constraint. `position` is the order on the boleto. A share item may name a criterion no unit has a fraction in yet: the issuance checks it.
- Shares are computed by the item (`BillingPlanItem.amounts_by_unit`): the total times the unit's fraction in the item's criterion (`UnitApportionmentFraction` rows; none counts as 0), rounded half-up to the cent, the leftover cents to the unit with the largest fraction (then by name), so the shares of all active units add up to the total. `uses_inconsistent_apportionment(totals)` says whether the named criterion does not close at 1 (totals by criterion id from `CondominiumApportionmentCriterion.objects.with_total()`; a criterion no unit has counts).
- **`BillingDocument`** (*DocumentoCobranca*): a unit's boleto as a record. `unit` and `payer` (a `Person` of the same company) are `PROTECT`; `reference_month` and `competence_month` are on day 1; `status` is `issued` → `registered` → `paid`, or `cancelled` (`BillingDocument.ALLOWED_NEXT_STATUSES`; paid and cancelled are final). **Overdue is derived, never stored**: `is_overdue_on(date)` and `objects.overdue_on(date)` (still awaiting payment and due before the date). A unit has at most one non-cancelled document per `reference_month` (partial unique constraint), so a cancelled one can be issued again. The late-payment and discount terms (`discount_*`, `late_fee_percentage`, `initial_late_fee_*`, `interest_*`, `monthly_interest_percentage`) are copied from `BillingRules` and from the `LateInterestRate` in force on the due date when it is issued, so changing the rules later does not alter it. Everything but the status is frozen after issuance, and the amount equals the sum of the items (`BillingDocumentRules`). The bank or gateway fields are not here (phase 5).
- **`BillingDocumentItem`** (*DocumentoCobrancaItem*): `position`, `description`, `cost_center` (of the unit's condominium) and `entry_template` (of that cost center; the entry the payment will post), both `PROTECT`, positive `amount`. Active cost center and template are required only when the item is created; a saved item never changes.

## API (`/api/`)
`billing-plans`, company-scoped: list (`?condominium=`, `?active=`, `?query=` on the name), create, retrieve, update, delete (its items go with it). A plan is written **with its `items`** (at least one), all or none (`services/create_billing_plan.py`, `update_billing_plan.py`, `replace_billing_plan_items.py`): sending `items` replaces the whole list in the given order; a PATCH without `items` keeps them. An item error is reported as `items.<n>.<field>`: the nested serializer runs the item rules without the plan, then `BillingPlanSerializer` asks `BillingPlan.collect_item_errors` to run them again with the plan candidate attached (the cost center's condominium). Responses carry `condominium_name` and, per item, `cost_center_name` and `entry_template_name`. Both models are audited; the Admin shows a plan with its items inline.

`condominium-billing-rules` (`?condominium=`; create takes `condominium` and the rule fields, one set per condominium, the condominium of a saved set cannot change) and `late-interest-rates` (`?condominium=`, newest start first), both company-scoped (another company's id is 404; as a related id, 400 `does_not_exist`). The condominium payload no longer carries `billing_rules`: the form creates the condominium and then its rules.

`billing-documents`, company-scoped and **read only** besides cancel, reissue, PDF and the `extra` action (spec 054, below): list (`?kind=monthly|extra`, `?condominium=`, `?unit=`, `?payer=`, `?status=`, `?reference_month=`, `?competence_month=`, `?is_overdue=`, `?query=` on unit and payer name) and retrieve (with `items`, names of condominium, unit and payer, and `is_overdue`), plus `POST /{id}/cancel/` (an issued or registered document; anything else is a 400 `billing_document.status.transition_not_allowed`). Documents are created only by `services/create_billing_document.py` (`NewBillingDocument`: the amount is the sum of the items, the terms are copied, all or none; the condominium must have billing rules, which the monthly issuance checks first) and cancelled by `services/cancel_billing_document.py`. Both models are audited; the Admin is read-only, with the items inline.

## Report: Inadimplência (spec 052)
`GET /api/billing-documents/delinquency/` (`?condominium=`, `?reference_month=`, company-scoped like the viewset) answers the **overdue** documents (`issued` or `registered`, due date before today) with `as_of`, `count`, `total_amount`, `by_condominium` and `rows` (condominium, unit, payer, reference month, due date, `days_overdue`, face amount; by condominium then oldest due date; at most 2000, `truncated` says if more while the totals cover all). `delinquency/pdf/` is the same report as an `application/pdf` attachment. `services/build_delinquency_report.py` builds it (the amount is the boleto's face value: no fine or interest, which only the gateway computes on payment) and `services/render_delinquency_pdf.py` renders `billing/delinquency_report.html` through `core/infrastructure/pdf.py` (`render_pdf`: WeasyPrint that refuses every URL; the boleto PDF uses it too). The money and date text of PDFs and e-mails is in `core/infrastructure/formatting.py`.

## Extra boleto (spec 054)
A **standalone boleto outside the monthly issuance**, for one or more units of a condominium, with its own due date. It is a `BillingDocument` with `kind = extra` (`monthly` is the default): the one-document-per-unit-and-month constraint applies only to `monthly` documents, so a unit can have several extras beside its monthly boleto, and the monthly issuance ignores them when it checks what was already issued. `POST /api/billing-documents/extra/` takes `condominium`, `due_date` (not in the past: a `BillingDocumentRules` rule), `competence_month` (first day; it is also the `reference_month`), `units` and `lines`. A line is written like a launch of *Inclusão de valores* (event, description, allocation individual/collective/fraction with a criterion, sub-conta, amount): the serializer builds an unsaved `BillingEntry` (recurrence `once`) per line and runs its rules, so an event of the administradora (the registration fee: the staff adds it as a line) has no sub-conta and any other line needs one, and the errors come back as `lines.<n>.<field>`. `services/issue_extra_billing_document.py` skips, and reports in `skipped`, the units that are inactive or billing-blocked, have no payer or (active gateway) a payer without tax id, splits the lines among the units that can be issued (the manager-unit exemption does not apply: the staff chose the unit), and issues each unit in its own transaction through `create_billing_document`; the PDF draft and the gateway registration are scheduled on commit by `schedule_billing_document_tasks` (shared with the monthly issuance). The answer is 201 when something was issued, 200 when nothing was; `?kind=monthly|extra` filters the list.

## Monthly issuance (spec 026)
`services/issue_condominium_billing.py` issues a condominium's reference month and records a **`BillingRun`** (condominium, `reference_month`, `status` `running` / `succeeded` / `partial` / `failed`, `issued_count`, `already_issued_count`, `skipped_count`, `failed_count`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, and `problems`: a list of `{unit, code}` with `unit` null for a whole-condominium problem). It uses the items of every plan in force (`BillingPlan.objects.in_force`), in plan and position order, and `BillingRules` for the due date (`due_date_of`), the competence month and the terms.
- **Billed units:** active, not `is_billing_blocked`, and not the manager unit when `CondominiumBillingRules.exempts_manager_unit`. A line of zero is omitted; a unit left with no line is skipped (`nothing_to_bill`). The payer is `Unit.billing_payer(today)`: the current tenant when `Unit.billing_recipient` is `tenant` and there is one, else the current owner with the largest share (the earliest link on a tie); none means `no_current_payer`.
- **Isolation and idempotency:** each unit is issued in its own transaction (`create_billing_document`); a document the model refuses is `failed` (`document_invalid`) and the run is `partial`. A unit that already has a non-cancelled document for the month is counted in `already_issued_count`, so a rerun adds nothing. `no_billing_rules`, `no_plan_in_force` and `inconsistent_apportionment` fail the whole run (`failed`) with nothing issued.
- **Problems in the API:** each entry of `problems` is answered as `{unit, unit_name, code}`; `unit_name` is `Unit.display_name`, the unit's block and number (`BillingRun.problems_with_unit_names`, one query per run) and is null for a whole-condominium problem; the stored JSON stays `{unit, code}`. The Cobrança screens (specs 028–029) turn the codes into Portuguese sentences and links.
- **Triggers:** `tasks.issue_due_billing` (beat, daily at 06:00 `TIME_ZONE`) enqueues `tasks.issue_condominium_billing_task` for each active condominium whose `CondominiumBillingRules.issuance_day` is today, with the current month as the reference month. `POST /api/billing-runs/` {`condominium`, `reference_month` on day 1} runs the same service synchronously and answers the run; `GET /api/billing-runs/` (`?condominium=`, `?reference_month=`, `?status=`) and `GET /api/billing-runs/{id}/` read it. Runs cannot be edited or deleted; the Admin is read-only and the model is audited.

## Draft boleto PDF (spec 027)
**`BillingDocumentPdf`** is the current draft PDF of a document (one per document; the file lives in the bucket, `file_key` is built from the company id, document id and a random id). `services/render_billing_document_pdf.py` renders `infrastructure/rendering/templates/billing/boleto_draft.html` (found through `TEMPLATES["DIRS"]` in the settings) with WeasyPrint, whose `url_fetcher` refuses every URL, so rendering never touches the network or the file system; `infrastructure/rendering/boleto_template.py` shapes what the page prints (Brazilian money, CPF/CNPJ and date formats, the discount/fine/interest sentences taken from the terms copied onto the document). The page is Portuguese, carries the "RASCUNHO" watermark and "Sem valor para pagamento", and has no barcode or digitable line (a box says they appear once the title is registered); the administrator is shown by name (no logo yet, `known-gaps.md`).
- **Generation:** `services/generate_billing_document_pdf.py` renders, uploads with `ObjectStorage.put_object` and saves or replaces the row; the replaced file is deleted after the commit (a failed delete leaves an orphan). The issuance enqueues `tasks.generate_billing_document_pdf_task` (by name, `transaction.on_commit`; retries with backoff) for each document it creates; a PDF failure never undoes or blocks an issuance.
- **API:** `GET /api/billing-documents/{id}/pdf/` answers `{download_url, expires_in, generated_at}` (a signed URL valid 300 s) or a 404 `billing_document_pdf.not_generated`; `POST` generates or replaces it now (201 the first time, 200 after). An unconfigured bucket is the usual 503. Needs Pango and `fonts-liberation` (`architecture/operations.md`).



## Billing entries: *Inclusão de valores* (spec 039)
The values launched for a condominium's boletos **besides the plan**; the monthly issuance adds the launches in force to each unit's boleto after the plan items (a month with launches and no plan is issued).
- **`BillingEvent`**: `company`, `code` (unique per company), `name`, `active`. It only fills the description (code 250 = "Limpeza do condomínio"); it is **not** tied to a cost center. API `billing-events` (company-scoped CRUD, `?active=`, `?query=`); deleting one that launches use is refused (`BillingEvent.delete`, code `billing_event.deletion.has_entries`).
- **`BillingEntry`** (*Lançamento*): `condominium` (fixed), `event` (optional), `description`, `allocation` `individual` / `collective` / `fraction` (a fraction entry names an `apportionment_criterion` of the same condominium, like a share item of a plan), `recurrence` `once` / `installments` (`installments_total` ≥ 2) / `always` (`end_month` stops it), `start_month`, `cost_center` (the sub-conta of the same condominium, active) and `entry_template` (**the cost center's only active template** (`CostCenter.sole_active_entry_template`), taken by `BillingEntry.clean`; none or several is the error `billing_entry.cost_center.without_one_entry_template`), `amount`. **`BillingEntryUnit`** is a selected unit, with its own `amount` only for an individual entry. Rules: `models/rules/billing_entry.py`, `billing_entry_unit.py`; the nested units are checked against the entry by `BillingEntry.collect_unit_errors`.
- **Allocation:** individual gives each selected unit the amount (or its own); collective splits the total equally; fraction splits it in proportion to the selected units' fraction in the entry's criterion (`UnitApportionmentFraction`; normalised by their sum, so a unit without one is refused: `billing_entry.unit.without_fraction`). A split is rounded half-up to the cent and the leftover goes to one unit (the largest weight, then by name), so the parts always add up to the total (`BillingEntry.amounts_by_unit`).
- **Recurrence:** `BillingEntry.covers(month)` / `BillingEntry.objects.in_force(month)` (same rule in SQL): once → its month; installments → `installments_total` consecutive months from `start_month` (`installment_label` = `k/n`); always → from `start_month` until `end_month`.
- **Line on the boleto:** `line_description(month)` = `<event code in 4 digits>-<event name>` (`0052-CONDOMINIO`), then " - description" if there is one, plus " (k/n)" for installments, 120 characters at most. A **plan item** may name an event too (`BillingPlanItem.event`; it needs an event or a description, never an event of the administradora; `line_description()`). The item keeps `billing_entry` (nullable FK): a launch **a document was issued from is frozen** (`is_used`): it cannot be changed or deleted, only ended (`POST /{id}/end/`, `end_month` ≥ start).
- **API `billing-entries`:** CRUD with nested `units` (replaces the selection, all or none: `services/create_billing_entry.py`, `update_billing_entry.py`, `replace_billing_entry_units.py`); `?condominium=`, `?event=`, `?cost_center=`, `?allocation=`, `?recurrence=`, `?reference_month=` (keeps the launches the month carries and adds `month`: line, installment and each unit's amount); `POST /preview/?reference_month=` (`services/preview_billing_entry.py`: nothing is saved); `POST /{id}/end/` (`services/end_billing_entry.py`).
- **Issuance:** `issue_condominium_billing` reads `BillingEntry.objects.in_force(month)` of the condominium, computes each entry over its *active* selected units (`BillingEntry.amounts_for_selected_units`) and appends one `NewBillingDocumentItem` per unit with the entry's cost center and template. The registration fee is a launch too (spec 038 below).

## Registration fee, the administradora's own credit (spec 038)
The administradora charges a **bank registration fee** on every boleto (today R$ 3.90; it pays the gateway less) and **it must never be credited to a condominium**. The staff launches it like any value (`0005-TARIFA BANCO 3,90` on the boleto), with an event marked **`BillingEvent.credits_company`** ("Receita da imobiliária").
- **A launch with such an event has no sub-conta** (`BillingEntryRules`: required for any other event, refused for this one) and its document line has **no cost center and no entry template** and `credits_company` true (a database constraint: both empty or both set). The monthly issuance builds it like any launch line.
- **Payment:** `post_billing_document_payment` credits the condominium lines to its current account as before and each company line to a **`CompanyEntry`** (finance; the administradora's own ledger; unique per document line; immutable). `GET /api/company-entries/` (date filters `entry_date__gte/lte`, `?query=`) and `GET /api/company-entries/balance/` read it; the screen is Financeiro → Conta da imobiliária.
- **One fee per document:** a second copy (reissue) registers a new charge for the same document, which keeps its lines, so the fee is charged once.
- **Discount, multa, interest:** the early-payment discount never applies to the fee: with a fee on the boleto the gateway discount is sent as a **fixed** amount over the other lines (`infrastructure/gateway/charges.py::_discount`; the PDF says "sobre o valor sem a tarifa"); without one it stays a percentage. The **multa and the interest are over the whole boleto, the fee included**.
- **Not built:** payment at the administradora's counter (*sem taxa banco*; it has a cash desk), and the reversal of a company entry on a refund.

## Gateway account (spec 037)
**`GatewayAccount`** is the company's single Asaas account (one per `Company`, `PROTECT`): it issues the boletos of **every** condominium and receives all the money. The condominiums have no account at the gateway and need no CNPJ; what each one is owed exists only in the ledger. Fields: `status` (`pending` → `active` / `blocked`) and `api_key` / `webhook_token` in `EncryptedTextField` (`core/infrastructure/fields/encrypted_text.py`; Fernet, key `GATEWAY_CREDENTIALS_KEY`). The credentials are excluded from the audit log, the Admin form and every message, and there is **no API endpoint**. Spec 037 replaced the sub-account per condominium of spec 030 (migrations `0011`–`0012` move an existing account to the company of its condominium and refuse two accounts of one company).
- **Set-up:** `manage.py setup_gateway_account <company_id> [--rotate-webhook-token] [--register-webhook EMAIL]` asks for the Asaas key without echoing it, calls `services/set_up_gateway_account.py` (it checks the key with `GET /myAccount/status`, stores it encrypted, generates the webhook token once and sets the account `active`; a refused key stores nothing, code `gateway_account.api_key.invalid`). Running it again replaces only the key. `--rotate-webhook-token` calls `services/rotate_gateway_webhook_token.py`. `--register-webhook` calls `services/register_gateway_webhook.py`, which posts the events to `ASAAS_WEBHOOK_URL` with the account's token (it updates a webhook already registered for the same URL instead of duplicating it); without it the command prints the token to paste in the Asaas panel.
- **Where each boleto's money goes in the ledger:** every `BillingDocumentItem` carries its `cost_center` and `entry_template`, so one boleto can credit the current account and, for example, a works fund; spec 032 posts one credit per item when the payment arrives.
- **Client:** `infrastructure/gateway/asaas_client.py` (`urllib3`, 15 s timeout, no automatic retry; a 429, a 5xx or a network failure raise `GatewayError` with `gateway_account.gateway.unavailable`, a 4xx `gateway_account.gateway.rejected`).
- `GatewayAccount.can_register_charges()`: only an `active` account registers charges; a company without one leaves the issued documents as drafts and the run reports `gateway_account_missing`.
- **Sandbox:** the status read and the Pix key creation by API (`POST /pix/addressKeys`, type `EVP`) work with the existing CPF account; the webhook registration, a charge and the confirmed payment are still to be validated (spec 037, *Pending before done*).

## Charge registration (spec 031)
After a unit's document commits, the issuance enqueues `tasks.register_billing_document_task` when the condominium's company has an `active` `GatewayAccount`; the service `services/register_billing_document.py` then registers it as an Asaas BOLETO charge on the company's account (the document becomes `registered`).
- **Records:** `GatewayCustomer` (one per person and account, so a person is one customer across every condominium of the company; looked up by `externalReference`, then `cpfCnpj`, before creating; Asaas notifications off) and `BillingDocumentRegistration` (every attempt kept: one `active` per document, `replaced`, or `failed` with the gateway's error codes; the charge's id is unique). It stores the boleto URL, our number, digitable line, barcode, Pix payload and expiry; the Pix QR image is drawn from the payload where it is shown.
- **Charge:** the document id is the `externalReference`; value and due date are the document's; fine, monthly interest and discount come from the terms stored on the document (`early_payment_discount_days` is days **before** the due date, sent as `dueDateLimitDays`); the first-days fine, the grace period and `interest_calculation` have no effect at the gateway. `daysAfterDueDateToRegistrationCancellation` comes from `ASAAS_REGISTRATION_LIFETIME_BUSINESS_DAYS` (default 60) because it cannot change afterwards.
- **Idempotency and failures:** a document with an active registration gets it back; a charge already at the gateway (found by `externalReference`) is reused after a lost response. A gateway outage (`GatewayUnavailable`: timeout, 429, 5xx) is retried with backoff and leaves the document `issued`; a refusal becomes a `failed` registration and is not retried. A missing Pix QR keeps the boleto.
- **Issuance notes:** with an active account a unit whose payer has no CPF/CNPJ (`Person.tax_id`) is skipped with `payer_without_document`; without an active account the documents are still issued as drafts and the run records `gateway_account_missing` (no unit).
- **API:** `billing-documents` list and detail carry `registration` (null or `{id, registered_due_date, our_number, digitable_line, barcode, boleto_url, pix_payload, pix_expires_at, created_at}`); the gateway ids and credentials never appear.
- **Not yet validated against the sandbox** (it needs a CNPJ sandbox account): the real responses of the customer, charge, `identificationField` and `pixQrCode` calls, the interest counting and the Pix QR without a key.

## Payment webhook and ledger posting (spec 032)
`POST /api/webhooks/asaas/` (no JWT, not in the OpenAPI schema) authenticates with the `asaas-access-token` header (the account's `webhook_token`, compared in constant time), stores a **`GatewayEvent`** (unique `event_id`; payload kept as received; a repeat is answered 200 and not stored again) and enqueues `process_gateway_event_task` after the commit. Malformed body → 400, wrong token → 401, nothing stored.
- **Processing** (`services/process_gateway_event.py`, once per event): `PAYMENT_RECEIVED` looks the charge up by its gateway id in the account's registrations. Active registration + `registered` document + paid `value` equal to the document's amount → `services/post_billing_document_payment.py`: one credit per item in the condominium's current account (the item's cost center and entry template, unit and competence month of the document, dated by `paymentDate`, a bank batch), a **`BillingDocumentPayment`** (date, amount, net amount) and the document `paid`, in one transaction. Other events are `ignored` (stored; part 05 acts on the cancellations).
- **Outcomes:** `processed`, `ignored` (`event_not_handled`, `already_paid`) or `unprocessed` with a reason: `unknown_charge`, `charge_not_active`, `document_not_registered`, `invalid_payload`, `amount_mismatch` (no tolerance margin: interest or fine make the value differ, and that case is resolved by staff) or `posting_refused` (a ledger rule refused, the codes are in `error_codes`; nothing is posted). The **unprocessed queue is the Admin list** of `GatewayEvent` filtered by outcome; its "Reprocess" action runs an event again.
- **Ledger:** `CurrentAccountEntry` gained the origin `automatic_billing`, a unique `billing_document_item` (one entry per item) and an optional `created_by` (allowed only for that origin); see `apps/finance.md`.
- **Not validated against the sandbox yet** (needs a CNPJ sandbox account and a tunnel to the endpoint).

## Daily reconciliation (spec 033)
`tasks.reconcile_gateway_accounts` (beat, daily 00:30) runs `services/reconcile_gateway_account.py` for each `active` account over `GATEWAY_RECONCILIATION_WINDOW_DAYS` (3). It lists the payments Asaas received in the window (`AsaasClient.list_received_payments`, all pages) and, for each: a payment whose document is already `paid` is only counted; any other goes through `process_gateway_event` as a `GatewayEvent` with the id `reconciliation:<payment id>` (so a second run neither posts nor stores it again). Each run is a **`GatewayReconciliationRun`** (window, found / already posted / processed / unprocessed counts, status; a gateway outage fails that run with its error code and the other accounts go on). By hand: `manage.py reconcile_gateway --days N` (use a longer window after a webhook outage; Asaas keeps undelivered events 14 days). The unprocessed queue is the Admin list of `GatewayEvent`.
- **Not validated against the sandbox yet:** the `paymentDate[ge]` / `paymentDate[le]` filters and the paging.

## Overdue handling and reissue (spec 034)
No automatic reissue: while the registration lives Asaas applies the fine and interest the charge was created with (its lifetime is `ASAAS_REGISTRATION_LIFETIME_BUSINESS_DAYS`). Staff reissue by hand: `POST /api/billing-documents/{id}/reissue/` with `due_date` (not in the past) runs `services/reissue_billing_document.py` for a `registered` document that has an `active` or `cancelled` registration: it creates a new charge (reference `<document id>:<n>`, same terms, the document amount is not recalculated), marks the old registration `replaced`, saves the new one as `active` with the reason `reissue`, and only then cancels the old charge at the gateway (`DELETE /payments/{id}`); if that fails the old registration carries `old_charge_not_cancelled` and a payment of it lands in the unprocessed queue. A refusal of the new charge is a `failed` `reissue` registration and the current one stays; a gateway outage answers 503 and changes nothing. Nothing is ever deleted.
- **Events:** `PAYMENT_BANK_SLIP_CANCELLED` / `PAYMENT_DELETED` mark the active registration `cancelled` (the document stays `registered`; the API shows `registration: null` until a reissue); the same event for a replaced registration (our own cancellation) is ignored. `PAYMENT_REFUNDED` of a paid document is `unprocessed` (`payment_refunded`): staff reverses the ledger entries. The second copy of a boleto is the active registration's `boleto_url`.
- **Not validated against the sandbox yet:** the delete call and these events.

## E-mail delivery (spec 035)
When a registration commits (the first one or a reissue), `tasks.send_billing_document_email_task` e-mails the payer through Resend (`infrastructure/gateway/resend_client.py`, `urllib3`; `RESEND_API_KEY` and the verified sender `RESEND_FROM`): the text is `infrastructure/rendering/delivery_email.py` (Portuguese, a **link** to the boleto, the digitable line and the Pix copy-and-paste, everything escaped; no attachment; Asaas's own notifications stay off). **`BillingDocumentDelivery`** is unique per registration and channel (`email` is the only one built), so a repeat cannot send twice; the provider's idempotency key is the delivery id. Outcomes: `sent` (with the provider's message id), or `failed` with `payer_without_email`, `email_not_configured` or `provider_rejected`; only an unavailable provider raises and is retried with backoff.
- **Provider webhook:** `POST /api/webhooks/resend/` (no JWT, not in the schema) checks the Svix signature with `RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (5-minute tolerance); `email.delivered` moves a `sent` delivery to `delivered`, `email.bounced` / `email.complained` fail it (`bounced`, `complained`); a failed delivery is never promoted.
- **API:** `billing-documents` carry `delivery` (the latest delivery of the active registration: `status`, `destination`, `sent_at`, `failure_reason`).
- **Not validated against Resend yet:** a real send and the webhook.

## Registration and payment on the screens (spec 036)
`billing-documents` also carry `payment` (`paid_on`, `amount`, `net_amount`; null until the document is paid, from `BillingDocumentPayment`). The document page shows the boleto (link, digitable line, Pix copy-and-paste), the payment, the e-mail delivery and, for a registered document, the "Reemitir boleto" action. The gateway account status and the unprocessed queue are Admin-only in this phase.
