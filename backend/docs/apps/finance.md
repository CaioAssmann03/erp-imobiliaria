# App `finance`

**State:** cost centers and entry templates, suppliers and bank accounts, accounts payable, and the ledger (current account, entry batches, entries, reversal, automatic posting on payment): API + Admin done (specs 009–015 in `features/history.md`). The screens are in the frontend (spec 016); the payment receipts (spec 018) are done and in use on the VPS. Depends on `condominiums` and `core`.

## Rules worth knowing (the fields are in `models/`; the error codes in `errors/`)
- **`CostCenter`** (the PDF's *SubConta*): a `code`, the number the staff knows it by (0 is the *Conta Normal*), unique per condominium; the next free one is assigned when it is left out (`CostCenter.next_code_in`, spec 039; the migrations number the existing ones in creation order). Its `condominium` never changes after creation (ledger entries group by it) and it cannot be deleted while it has entry templates (deactivate it).
- **`CompanyEntry`** (*LançamentoEmpresa*, spec 038): a credit of the **administradora itself** (the boleto registration fee), written when a boleto is paid, one per `BillingDocumentItem` that credits the company; immutable (no update, no delete); read-only Admin, audited. It never touches a condominium's current account. API `company-entries` (read-only, `entry_date__gte/lte`, `?query=`) and `company-entries/balance/`.
- **`EntryTemplate`** (*Histórico*): its `cost_center` never changes after creation. Ledger entries now point at it, so deleting a template that has entries is refused by the database (`PROTECT`) and answers 500 until it gets a delete rule (known gap 27).
- **`BankAccount`**: `company` is always set (the administrator that owns it); `condominium` is set only for a condominium's **own** account, the exception (boleto money lands in the company's account by default). There is no `Condominium.own_bank_account`: it would make `condominiums` depend on `finance`; which account receives a boleto is decided in phase 4.
- **`Supplier`**: 1:1 with `Person`; bank code, branch and account number are filled together or not at all (a PIX key may stand alone). Payment data is masked in the audit log and not searchable.
- **`AccountPayable`**: `condominium` empty means a company expense. A cost center is required with a condominium (and must be its own) and not allowed without. Only an `open` account changes; `paid` and `cancelled` are closed, and a paid one cannot be deleted. The payment fields are filled exactly when `paid`; `amount_paid` may differ from `amount`. The model's `clean()` checks that the account is open, then `AccountPayableRules` (`models/rules/account_payable.py`) holds the holder, payment and date rules. Amount and due-date history is the audit log.
- **`CurrentAccount`** (*ContaCorrente*): one per condominium (`condominium` must belong to the account's `company` and never changes); `balance` is a cache, not editable, starts at 0 and only the entry service changes it. A `rental_contract` holder comes with phase 8. **Audit-exempt** (`AUDIT_EXEMPT`): its balance derives from the ledger entries, which never change and carry their own `created_by`/`created_at`.
- **`EntryBatch`** (*Lote*): groups ledger entries and has no condominium (one batch may cover several). `origin` is `cash`, `extra_cash` or `bank`; `cash_number` (> 0) is required for the two cash origins and forbidden for `bank` (`EntryBatchRules` plus a `CheckConstraint`). Audited.
- **`CurrentAccountEntry`** (*LancamentoContaCorrente*): a line of a current account with `batch`, `entry_template`, `cost_center`, `entry_date`, `competence_month` (first day), `kind` (credit/debit), positive `amount`, `description`, `justification` (the reason of a reversal, required then; a manual entry does not need one, spec 051), `origin` (`manual` or `automatic_payable`), `account_payable` (only for the automatic origin, one entry per account payable), `unit` (optional; of the account's condominium), `reversal_of` (one-to-one: at most one reversal per entry), `created_by` (set by the service, not editable) and `registry` (one global counter from the PostgreSQL sequence `current_account_entry_registry_seq`, never reused). `CurrentAccountEntryRules` checks that account, batch, cost center and entry template agree on company and condominium, that cost center and template are active, the origin rules and the reversal rules (opposite kind, same amount and account, once, not a reversal). It **never changes and is never deleted** (`save()` of a saved entry and `delete()` raise a coded error; the Admin shows it read-only); a mistake is fixed by `services/reverse_entry.py`, which posts the opposite entry. Audit-exempt.
- **`post_entry`** (`services/post_entry.py`, stage B): the only code that changes `CurrentAccount.balance`. In one `transaction.atomic` it locks the account row (`select_for_update`), runs `entry.full_clean()`, saves the entry and adds `entry.signed_amount` (credit `+amount`, debit `-amount`) to the balance. An invalid entry saves nothing and leaves the balance untouched; a negative balance is allowed. The API creates only manual entries through it. `CurrentAccount.recalculated_balance()` sums the entries with their sign and never writes: it is the check that the cached `balance` still matches the ledger.

- **`PaymentReceipt`** (spec 018): a PDF/JPEG/PNG of at most 10 MB attached to a **paid** account payable (several per account); the file is in the R2 bucket, the row holds `file_key` (built by the service, never from the file name), `original_name`, `content_type`, `size_bytes`, `uploaded_by` and `confirmed_at` (empty until the file is checked in the bucket; only confirmed receipts are listed). `PaymentReceiptRules` checks the account is paid; the type and size are field validators. Audited.

**Life cycle (`services/`)**: `settle_account_payable(account, AccountPayablePayment(...))` and `cancel_account_payable(account)`. Both lock the stored row (`AccountPayable.lock_for_change()`, which refuses anything but `open`), then `full_clean()`. `AccountPayablePayment` carries `settled_by` (the user). Settling an expense **of a condominium** also posts its debit (`services/post_account_payable_entry.py`) in the same transaction: `amount_paid` on the payment date, competence month of the due date, description `"<supplier> - <cost center>"`, in a new bank batch dated on the payment day, in the condominium's current account (created on the first posting). A company expense posts nothing. `AccountPayable.entry_template` (from its own cost center) is required to pay a condominium expense and not allowed on a company expense.

## API
Flat, company-scoped resources: `cost-centers`, `entry-templates`, `suppliers` (the response carries a read-only `person_detail`), `bank-accounts` (`company` is the user's, read-only), `accounts-payable` (`POST /{id}/settle/` and `/cancel/`; `status` and the payment fields are read-only in the regular serializer), `current-accounts` (create, list, retrieve; the balance is read-only), `entry-batches` (create, list, retrieve, update; no delete) and `current-account-entries` (the statement, newest first: create a manual entry, list with filters, retrieve, `POST /{id}/reverse/`; an entry is never updated or deleted; rows carry the condominium, template, cost center and unit names, the batch origin and date, and `is_reversed`). `POST /accounts-payable/{id}/receipts/` (file name, type and size → an unconfirmed receipt and a signed `PUT` URL; the browser uploads straight to R2) and `payment-receipts` (list with `?account_payable=`, retrieve, `POST /{id}/confirm/` which checks the real size in the bucket and deletes a missing or wrong-sized file, `GET /{id}/download-url/` for a short-lived signed URL, `DELETE`): services `create_payment_receipt`, `confirm_payment_receipt`, `delete_payment_receipt`. `GET /current-accounts/{id}/balances/` answers the balance of the account and of each cost center (from the entries, via `CurrentAccountEntryQuerySet`). `POST /entry-batches/{id}/entries/` posts several manual entries into the batch, all or none (`services/post_entries.py`; errors are named `entries.<n>.<field>`). Responses of accounts payable carry `condominium_name`, `cost_center_name` and `entry_template_name`. Filters and search: see the viewsets and the OpenAPI schema. Another company's record is 404; as a related id it is 400 `does_not_exist`. A model rule raised outside a serializer (a delete rule) becomes the usual 400 through `api_exception_handler`.

## Used by
The `core` dashboard counts active condominiums without cost centers, and its accounts payable section reads `AccountPayableQuerySet`; `seed_demo` creates three cost centers per condominium.

## Automatic billing entries (spec 032)
A paid billing document posts one **credit** per `BillingDocumentItem` with the origin `automatic_billing`: no user (`created_by` is null; the constraint `current_account_entry_created_by_required_unless_automatic_billing` allows that only for this origin), `billing_document_item` set (unique: an item is posted once; `…matches_origin` ties the origin to the field) and the item's cost center and entry template. Built by `CurrentAccountEntry.receiving(item, current_account, batch, paid_on)`; posted by billing's `post_billing_document_payment` through `post_entries`. The statement and the API show the entry like any other (`created_by` may be null).
