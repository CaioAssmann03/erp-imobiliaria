# Public pages design system (N — Norms)

The public pages (`app/(marketing)`) share the ERP tokens (`app/globals.css`) but have their own voice: an editorial
the same font as the ERP (Inter, the default; no serif or mono), larger titles, uppercase labels, square buttons and orange as an accent. They use the orange
accent: the layout wrapper has the `theme-public` class (end of `app/globals.css`), which fixes only the orange accent, the Inter font and
the error red for the public subtree, so the user's saved ERP accent and font (`data-accent`, `data-font` on `<html>`) never
leak in and nothing switches after hydration. **The mode is shared with the ERP:** surfaces and ink follow `data-mode` on `<html>`
(light, dark or system, the same cookie `ui_mode` and the same variables as the ERP; there is no second dark theme). The header's `ModeToggle`
(`components/ModeToggle.tsx`) switches it and the ERP opens in the same mode. Canvases that read colors from tokens at mount re-read them on a mode change
(`subscribeMode` in `lib/theme/mode.ts`).

## Type

| Use | Class | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Page title (hero, `PageIntro`) | `font-display text-hero` | Inter, weight 500 (the home hero adds `font-bold`, like the login title), `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 4.25rem)` |
| Section title | `font-display text-section` | `clamp(2rem, 3.4vw, 2.75rem)` |
| Item title (lists, steps) | `font-display text-title font-normal` | |
| Body and leads | `text-body text-ink-soft` | Inter, as in the ERP |
| Labels: nav, buttons, eyebrows, numbers | `text-label` | Inter 500, 11px, uppercase, `0.08em` tracking |

`font-display` (now just Inter, kept as a name for the big titles), `text-hero`, `text-section`, `text-label` and `rounded-sharp` are defined in section 5 of `app/globals.css`.

## Color

- Background `bg-canvas`; text `text-ink`, secondary `text-ink-soft`, faint `text-ink-faint`.
- **Orange (`brand`) is an accent only:** eyebrows (`text-label text-brand`), list numbers, the skyline illustration,
  progress lines, the METRI logo in the header (`components/brand/MetriLogo.tsx`, from `public/LOGO_METRI_TEXT.svg`: orange house + the name in the text color, or all in the text color with `mono` (unused for now); the header
  serves the landing and the login; `Brand` uses it too, and its compact form is the house alone, `HouseLogo`). Never a button fill, never a full title.
- Inverted band: the footer is `band bg-band text-ink-foreground` (`--color-band`: black in light mode, `#21211f` in dark mode, only a step above the canvas, never white; the `.band` class in `globals.css` flips `--color-ink`/`--color-ink-foreground` inside it in dark mode so the text stays light and the inverse buttons stay light-on-dark), with secondary text at `text-ink-foreground/65`. The sides of the `/how-it-works` buildings use `bg-band` to match it.
- The skyline's tones and the login logo's come from the same ramp, `brandRamp` in `lib/theme/brand-ramp.ts`: change it there, not in each canvas.
- Read colors from tokens, including in `<canvas>` (`getComputedStyle(...).getPropertyValue("--color-brand")`).

The screen rises when the section enters; under `lg` the image goes on top of the text) → a short invitation (a line in the item type, "Cada parte do sistema, com calma.", and a square primary `MarketingButton` "Conhecer os recursos" to `/features`, centered, `Reveal`) → a closing skyline (`ScanlineSkyline variant="footer"` at the end of the home only: same orange scanline buildings and lit windows as the hero but another silhouette, a hill (tall in the middle, low at the ends; `shape: "hill"` in `skyline-scene.ts`), seated on the footer's top edge to close the cycle; the buildings rise when it scrolls into view, not on load; the `-mb-12` cancels the footer's `mt-12`) → the footer (`SiteFooter`, shared by all public pages and the only closing block: there is no separate contact band; a full-bleed `bg-ink` footer, the brand, a one-line description, the "Agendar demonstração"/"Entrar" buttons and three link columns: site, modules (from `FEATURES`) and access; a hairline and the copyright below; only real routes and facts, no invented address, phone or social links). Each section must be understood at a glance.
- **Login to panel transition:** on a successful login `LoginForm` calls `startLoginTransition()` (`lib/auth/login-transition.ts`) and `components/LoginTransition.tsx`, mounted in the root layout so it survives the page change,
  covers the screen (`bg-canvas`) with the house logo building itself bottom-up in 16 horizontal bands that slide in from the left, then breathing slowly. It leaves in a fade when the
  path is no longer `/login` and at least 1.7 s have passed (10 s at most). With reduced motion the logo shows whole and still.
- **Sky flyers:** `hero/SkyFlyers.tsx` sends a plane (with a fading contrail) or a V-shaped flock of birds across the hero sky above the title every 6–13 s, drawn as the same orange scanlines (sprites are rows of segments on the 4px grid). It only draws while something is in the air, never when the tab is hidden or the hero is covered, and not at all in the lite mode (`isLite()`, see norms/design-system.md).
- **Hero to Summary transition (curtain):** the hero is `sticky top-0 h-svh` and everything after it lives in a
  `relative z-10 bg-canvas` wrapper in `page.tsx`, so the next section slides over the pinned hero and covers the
  buildings; the hero text fades out over the first 350px. No melt, no extra canvas. Keep the wrapper opaque.
- **Features page opening (`AcronymReveal`):** the first block of `/features` (which has no `PageIntro`: this block carries the page `h1`, screen-reader only): the name METRI as an acronym (Modelo Eficiente de Tempo e Recursos Imobiliários, from the README; data in `ACRONYM` in `content.ts`), in two timed phases that start when the stage is 30 % in view (`useInView`, once), everything centered in the middle of the stage: **1.** the word of the logo alone, without the house (`MetriLogo house={false}`); **2.** (at 1.4 s) it gives way to five stacked pills, one per word, pushed left and right in a zigzag and joined by straight bars ("necks", an SVG path in `neckPath` placed in the middle of the horizontal overlap of two pills, from the measured widths; the vertical geometry is computed, not read from `offset*`, which rounds to the pixel and leaves a step at the joint). Everything has square corners: no radius on the pills and no rounded fillets on the necks. The animation stops there: the pills are the final state. The group is centered with a measured `shift`. Everything is in `em` (1em = the stage text size, a `clamp` of the viewport) so it scales; the layout constants are at the top of the file. The "de" and "e" of the phrase are smaller and faint after their word. No eyebrow above it. Each row has an `aria-label` with the whole word. In the lite mode (which reduced motion turns on) it renders already as the pills.
- **Features page, one section per part of the system (`ModuleSection`):** after the acronym, each module gets a section (top padding only, `pt-32 md:pt-48`, so the gap above every hairline is the same: acronym to Condomínios, Condomínios to Lançamentos; the page wraps the sections in a `pb-32 md:pb-48` block for the space before the footer) with a hairline that draws across the top, then the picture of the module on the left (above the text on a phone) and, on the right, the name (no number or eyebrow) and a short text of two or three paragraphs, justified and hyphenated like `Summary` but smaller (`text-base`, `text-ink-soft`). Copy in `MODULES` in `content.ts` (only what the system does today, no invented figures); the picture is passed as `children`, so each module has its own, and **not** a building: the skyline is already the hero and the closing of the home. The first section is **Condomínios**, with `visuals/CondominiumPlan.tsx`: the registry hierarchy drawn plainly (a first treemap version was dropped: too hard to read). A bracket across the top is the condominium; under it three columns, each titled *Bloco A / B / C*, are the blocks; inside each, small numbered-by-hover squares, two per floor, are the units. On reaching the screen the bracket draws, the blocks appear left to right and each unit fills with the skyline's horizontal orange lines (3 px every 4; the square is 48/56 px inside its border so the lines close), in a wave; each block has its own tone from the accent tokens. The pointer dims the other blocks and the unit under it turns solid, and the caption below reads the path (idle "Condomínio › blocos › unidades", hover "Condomínio › Bloco B › Unidade 302"). The second section is **Lançamentos** (the billing entries, "Inclusão de valores"), with `placement="below"`: the title on top, the text under it across the full width in two justified, hyphenated columns (CSS columns, a paragraph never splits between them; one column under `lg`), and the picture underneath (`placement` is `left` by default, `right` or `below`; on a phone the picture is always above or below the text). Three earlier pictures were dropped (a flat orange bar chart, a boleto drawn in SVG with a barcode, a typographic accordion of the three recurrences): for now it has **no picture, text only**, as a test (six paragraphs in the two columns; `children` of `ModuleSection` is optional, and without it the section is just the title and the text). The last picture made for it, a **wide, restrained line chart in the style of a dashboard, no panel**, `visuals/EntryChart.tsx`, an example year in thousands of reais per month with two lines, *Lançado* (everything launched into the boletos, the brand orange, 2.25 px, with a 7 % flat orange area under it) and *Recorrente* (the part that repeats, installments and "todo mês": a faint dashed ink line). Fine horizontal grid (0, 2, 4, 6, 8 mil, labels at the left in faint ink), month names under it (initials under 640 px), no loose legend: the two names sit above the chart as a legend with a line sample, and show that month's values when the pointer is on it ("Mar" at the right instead of "exemplo"). It is drawn in **real pixels** (`ResizeObserver` on the wrapper, so the text stays sharp at any width; 320 px tall from `md`, 240 below) and the lines are a monotone cubic spline (`smoothLine`: smooth, never overshooting the data). On reaching the screen a clip rect reveals lines and area from left to right (1.9 s). **The pointer** (mouse or finger) snaps a hairline to the nearest month, with a ring on each line that glides between months (CSS transitions) and the legend values; idle it rests on December. Already drawn in the lite mode. The figures are only an example, labeled so. The third section is **Cobranças** (plans, the monthly issuance, the boleto with Pix and its follow-up), with `placement="right"` and `visuals/BillingFlow.tsx`: **no card, no controls, no text and no SVG: one huge number floating in 3D**, centered in its column. It counts up from `0` to `100%` once, the first time it reaches the screen (3.4 s, a decelerating ease, after a 0.4 s beat; digits turn like an odometer: `Digit` is a 0–9 column plus a closing 0 for 9→0, the units digit rolling continuously and the others only while the one below wraps; the hundreds and tens slots open in width and fade in as the number reaches them, so the whole stays centered), then stays on **100%** and only floats. The number has thickness (nine copies stacked in depth with `translateZ`, 3 px apart, darker toward the back: the brand orange mixed with `--color-window-off`) and the front face is the site's orange with the same scanlines as the skyline and the map (`repeating-linear-gradient`, 3 px clear and 1 px `--color-canvas`); it sways slowly in the air (`rotateX`/`rotateY`/`y` keyframes, 6.5 s, reversing, `perspective` on the parent, only while on screen) and a soft radial shadow on the ground follows it. Lite mode: already 100%, still and a little tilted. Earlier versions were dropped: columns with unit tags (too close to the Lançamentos panel), a stepped line chart, an odometer of the boleto amount with a label and a slider, and two digits counting the days 01–30. The next modules (pessoas, conciliação, contas a pagar, documentos) follow the same section with their own picture.
- **Prints in both modes (`ThemedImage`):** every screenshot of the system exists as a light and a dark image, and the page shows the one of the mode in use. `_components/ThemedImage.tsx` renders both `<Image>`s with the classes `.print-light` / `.print-dark` (end of `app/globals.css`): `html[data-mode="dark"]` hides the light one, any other mode hides the dark one, and `data-mode="system"` follows the OS through `prefers-color-scheme`; the hidden one is `display: none`, so it is lazy and never downloaded. Only the light one carries the `alt` (the dark one is `aria-hidden`). Used by `ScreenFrame` (home), `ScreenCollage` (its `screens` take `{ image, dark, alt }`, the lightbox included). To retake: the same screens, with the `ui_mode` cookie set to `dark`, at 1440 × 900 @2x.

- **A module page of its own (`/features/condominios`):** `story/CondominiumStory.tsx` is an **ordinary scrolling page read as one text**: no pinned stage, no scroll-driven text, no hairlines or big dividers between the parts. It opens with `story/StoryTitle.tsx` (the module name alone, enormous, bold and tight, each letter rising out of a mask when the page opens, like the home title; no eyebrow) and the lead as a large statement in ink (`text-xl md:text-[1.75rem]`). Then one single column (`max-w-2xl`) of running text from `MODULE_PAGES.condominios`: **large type** (`text-lg`, `md:text-[1.3rem]`, leading 1.7), **justified** and hyphenated, with only small left-aligned subheads (`font-display text-title font-medium`) between the parts, `pb-16 md:pb-20` apart. **Few photos, sometimes:** four of the six parts carry one (`PHOTOS_BY_SECTION`, alternating sides and sizes), `story/StoryPhoto.tsx`: an illustrative photo from `public/marketing/condominios` (the same set as the map cards, `globe/photos.ts`) seen through the scanline blind (3 px clear, 1 px `--color-canvas`), entering with a curtain from the top when it reaches the screen. **Always beside the text, never over or above it:** up to `xl` the photo floats at the side of the part's first paragraph (`float-left` / `float-right`, 32–38 % of the column, the text wraps around it; the part is a `flow-root`); from `xl` (1280 px) up it sits in the margin beside the column (`xl:absolute`, vertically centered on its part, sized in `vw` so it never touches the text) and drifts a little against the scroll (`useScroll` + `useTransform`, `drift` px, only in the margin); under `sm` (phones) it is hidden, since there is no room at the side. No SVG drawings, no icons: pictures only. At the end, a quick example of the system, `story/ScreenCollage.tsx`, **images only, no text and no pins**: **two real screens overlapped** (`unidades.png` large, `condominio.png` smaller in front, bottom right, both with a `-dark` twin, `shadow-float ring-1 ring-ink/10`) that drift at different speeds with the scroll (`useScroll`, opposite directions) and enter with a curtain and a slide. **A click on either one expands it** (`Lightbox`: a `theme-public` overlay portaled to `body` so the scroll transforms do not move it, `bg-canvas/95`, the image as large as the screen allows at 1.6:1, a "Fechar" button, closes with Esc or a click anywhere; `data-lenis-prevent` and non-passive wheel/touch listeners keep the page still behind it; it fades and scales in, no animation in the lite mode). Fictitious `seed_demo` data. Earlier versions were dropped: the two screens as plain stacked images with a caption each, and the overlap with four numbered pins, a legend and a line of text. The page ends with `ModuleOutro` (`sections/ModuleOutro.tsx`, on every module page): **as clean as possible**: no phrase or button (the demo call to action is in the footer), only a hairline and one row, narrow like the text column: a faint "Próximo" label, the name and one-line summary of the next page of the sequence (`NEXT_STEP`) and an arrow that slides on hover: Condomínios → Lançamentos → Cobranças → Como funciona (`/how-it-works`). ## New public page

Add the route under `app/(marketing)/`, export `metadata = pageMetadata({ title, description, path })` (`lib/site/page-metadata.ts`: canonical, Open Graph and Twitter in one) and add its path to `PUBLIC_PAGES` in `lib/site/public-pages.ts`: that single list feeds the sitemap and lets `proxy.ts` open the page without a login. Structured data goes in `<JsonLd>` (`_components/JsonLd.tsx`) and states only what the site already says.

## Don't

Pill buttons, `glass` panels, emoji or icon grids, invented metrics, gradient backgrounds, more than one orange
element competing in the same view.
